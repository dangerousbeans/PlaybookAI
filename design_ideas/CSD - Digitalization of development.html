Our innovation

the most innovation instruments for development digitalization

  1. Drones

  2. Cloud storage

  3. Scanning

  4. Mobile apps

  1. Overflight of the building by drones

    Thanks to the progress of aircraft technology and software, drones can be used at all stages of the life cycle of a building. Today, unmanned aerial vehicles perform several key functions, including: creating virtual models of buildings and structures, performing 3D scanning of existing structures, etc.

  2. Cloud storage

    Cloud storages provide extensive advantages for companies, as they make it possible to analyze large volumes of various data, extract significant value from them, as well as store records for past periods.

  3. Scanning

    3D laser scanning technology of buildings allows you to obtain data in the form of a point cloud about the size, position and configuration of the object. It is used both at the design stage and when restoring documentation for an existing building.

  4. Mobile apps

    Some of the functions are available to users in the mobile application, which allows you to monitor and control processes through your device.
